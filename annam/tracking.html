Monitor every surplus donation through its five lifecycle phases: ALERT, MATCH, SAFETY CHECK, DELIVER, and TRANSPARENCY.

About the 5-Stage Verification Architecture

1. ALERT Food donor logs surplus meals, cooking time, and safe consumption cutoffs.
2. MATCH Smart location match connects the nearest active volunteer and recipient NGO.
3. SAFETY CHECK Volunteer physically executes the mandatory 5-point FSSAI inspection checklist.
4. DELIVER Safe transit in thermal packaging with GPS courier monitoring.
5. TRANSPARENCY Shelter confirms receipt, certifying meal dignity and environmental carbon offset.
